Date: July 1, 2026 · by Ni Biashara · Organic / $0 · owner-published

## Why GBP is M&M's #1 free lever
M&M opened Nov 2025, has a thin review base, and — per its own site — is **"easy to miss from the road."** In an affluent, Maps-driven suburb, the Google page is where the visit decision is made. Photos drive ~+42% direction requests; reviews drive ranking and trust; posts keep the profile fresh. All free, all owner-published.

## ⚠️ Access first
Confirm M&M's GBP **ownership/manager status** before running the cadence (same pattern as the Google Workspace steps in Ola's welcome packet). Ni Biashara can draft; the owner publishes, or grants manager access. Nothing here is posted without that access + approval.

## Weekly post cadence (one every 2–3 days)
Rotate the four post types so the profile never goes stale. Each post = one photo + short copy + one button.

| Type | Use for | Button |
|---|---|---|
| **Offer** | Happy hour, catering trays, loyalty | Order online / Call |
| **Event** | Friday, karaoke, watch parties, private bookings | Reserve / Learn more |
| **What's new** | New dishes, "how to find us," hours | Order / Directions |
| **Product** | Individual menu items + price | Order / Menu |

All buttons point at **tracked paths** (`/go/mm-*`) so GBP traffic counts toward influenced sales.

## Review engine (urgent for a young venue)
Volume + rating is free ranking and trust — M&M's biggest near-term gap.
- **Ask at the counter / on the receipt:** "Enjoyed it? A 30-second Google review helps our new spot a lot — QR's right here." Generate a QR from the exact GBP review link.
- **Reply to every review**, good or bad, in owner voice. Templates:
  - *Positive:* "Thank you for coming out to M&M! Jollof and suya are waiting whenever you're back. 🙏"
  - *Critical:* "We appreciate the honest feedback and we're on it — we'd love the chance to make it right. Please reach us at (945) 327-0366."
- Target a steady climb in count and a 4.5+ rating.
