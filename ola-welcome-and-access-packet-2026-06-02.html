Control rules

How the work stays safe and manageable

These rules keep the project useful without turning it into a technical burden for the owner.

Owner control

M&M keeps final say on public-facing changes, key business accounts, and any spend or account transfer.

Technical handling

Ni Biashara handles implementation, testing, issue isolation, and rollout preparation.

Safe access

Private credentials should stay inside live account sessions. They should not be pasted into chats or shared documents.

What to use day to day

One review lane and one file lane

  • Use the shared tracker for status, requests, and approvals.
  • Use the shared files folder for menus, photos, and approved documents.
  • Use live account access only when a real account-level change is required.
What not to do

Avoid risky or confusing handoff habits

  • Do not send passwords or recovery codes in chat.
  • Do not split approvals across many random message threads.
  • Do not treat a public preview link as the account-control record.
