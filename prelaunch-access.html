Operating rhythm

The review cadence

A simple rhythm keeps the project visible without creating daily noise.
Review journey workflow
Review
Open the live site and current requests.
Check
Confirm what changed, what is blocked, and what needs approval.
Decide
Choose the next priority and lock the owner action items.
This week

Finish the missing access items

  • Confirm active public profiles and account owners.
  • Open the legacy website or domain access path.
  • Lock the immediate update list.
Weekly checkpoint

Short operating review

  • What changed on the live site
  • What requests were completed
  • What is blocked by access or approval
End of month

Business-value sync

  • What shipped
  • What the work improved
  • What next month should focus on
KPIs

What we will track and why it matters

These are the owner-facing signals that show whether the work is creating real business value.
KPI What it tells us How value is created How we will review it
Traffic Whether more people are reaching the website Better visibility and cleaner public links bring more qualified visits Weekly trend and top-page review
Calls / directions / order taps Whether the website is producing direct action Stronger conversion paths reduce drop-off and help turn attention into intent Checkpoint summary and month-end comparison
Lead forms Whether catering, event, and contact paths are being used Clearer inquiry flow helps capture business that might otherwise be lost Count and quality of inbound requests
Profile consistency Whether search, social, and public references are aligned Consistent public information reduces confusion and improves trust Checklist pass during weekly review
Ordering readiness How close the business is to a cleaner order and payment path Less manual handling and a clearer payment path support revenue growth Milestone status on access, tests, and launch decisions
What the weekly checkpoint should answer
  • What improved for customers this week?
  • What improved for owner visibility or control?
  • What is still blocked, and exactly why?
What the end-of-month sync should answer
  • What shipped and stayed live?
  • What measurable signals improved?
  • What should the next month prioritize for growth or operations?
