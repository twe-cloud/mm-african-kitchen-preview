## The business today (validated)
- **Concept:** M&M African Kitchen & Bar — **Nigerian-forward + fusion**, full bar, women/owner-operated by **Ola Oladoyin**. Opened **November 2025** (~8 months old as of this writing — young, thin review base still building).
- **Food:** jollof, suya, amala, pepper/egusi soups with fufu/poundo/eba, seafood, mini plates; fusion crossovers (Naija egg rolls, oxtail with rice & peas). Cocktails + **happy hour Tue–Fri 3–7 PM**. Dine-in, takeout, catering.
- **Assets already built:** loyalty program ("every dollar a point"), a performer funnel ("Play the room" — DJs, karaoke hosts, vocalists, comedians), an AI avatar host ("Chi-keh"), and catering/ordering pages.
- **Structural weakness — wayfinding:** the venue's own site says *"the address is easy to miss from the road."* Hidden retail-strip location on Teel Pkwy. Combined with a young review base, **first-visit confidence is the #1 conversion problem** — bigger here than at Shebelle.
- **Craig's arrangement:** Craig runs **M&M's Friday every week** ($100/night + 5%, details TBD). This is the mirror of DJ Twe's Sunday at Shebelle and the strategic center of gravity — see "The Friday wedge" below.

## Positioning shift
Lead with **"Naija done right — in the suburbs"** and **"the Friday the north side doesn't have."** Two doors into the same room: **(A)** the affluent African professional + family diaspora who want authentic West African food *without the 30–45 min drive downtown*; **(B)** the broader affluent Frisco foodie / date-night / group-dinner crowd curious about bold West African flavor. Sitting underneath both is the highest-margin, steadiest lane: **corporate/office catering** to Frisco–Plano's dense HQ campuses. Not "an African restaurant in Frisco" — *the* Naija table for the north metro, with a Friday nobody else up here is serving.

## The trade area

### A. Immediate (75033) — the highest-income ZIP in Frisco
The venue sits in **75033**: ~45,300 residents, **median household income ~$174,800**, **41.8% of households earn $200k+**, median age 36.1, 63% married, ~3.15 persons/household, **65% college-educated / 47% STEM degrees**, ~14.4% Asian, median home ~$652k, median rent ~$2,056. This is a **premium, educated, family-heavy, professional** trade area — money in pocket, but *not* a nightlife district. Best for **upscale family/group dining, date night, catering, and one strong weekly nightlife tentpole** (Friday), not a club-first frame.

Adjacent ZIPs feeding the venue:
- **75078 (Prosper):** ~$196k median, **48.8% earn $200k+** — the richest pocket, just north.
- **75035:** ~$166k, ~40% earn $200k+ — dense Frisco family growth.
- **75034 / 75036:** ~$111k–$152k, still affluent; 75034 borders Teel Pkwy directly.
- **Little Elm 75068:** ~$126k, younger, more diverse, more renters — the value/young-professional layer 10 min west.

**Apartment density near the venue** (young professional renters, repeatable crowd):
- **Alaqua at Frisco** (4770 Teel Pkwy) · **The Tyler** (16720 Doe Creek Rd) · **The Links on PGA Parkway** (15950 Paramount Way) and the PGA/Link District build-to-rent communities. 1BR ~$1,375–1,530.

### B. The diaspora — affluent and professional, spread across Collin County
Unlike Shebelle's lower-income Vickery Meadow hub, **M&M's diaspora is upmarket**:
- DFW is the **2nd-largest Nigerian population in Texas** — est. **25,000–35,000** (community leaders cite up to ~50,000 Igbo), concentrated in **Plano, Frisco, Irving, Garland**, drawn by tech/finance jobs, schools, and safe suburbs. Historically upper/middle class with high English fluency.
- **Plano is the community hub** — Nigerian churches, an active Nigerian professional association, and Nigerian restaurants. ~15 min from the venue.
- Organized institutions: **Edo Association of DFW** and other state/regional associations; Plano/Frisco Nigerian churches.
- → This is a diaspora that **can spend, caters office lunches, and hosts paid events.** Court it as affluent professionals, not as an enclave.

## The corporate-catering wedge (the highest-margin lane — lead with it)

Frisco–Plano is one of the densest corporate-HQ corridors in the country, and the back-to-office push is live. West African office-lunch and tray catering into a workforce that *already includes thousands of Nigerian professionals* is warm demand, recurring, and high-margin.

**Frisco employers (minutes away):** TIAA (new 500,000 sq ft at The Star), **PGA of America HQ**, T-Mobile, Thomson Reuters, **Keurig Dr Pepper**, Careington International, HCA (Center for Clinical Advancement), Quality Custom Distribution, **ThyssenKrupp Elevator HQ**, 200+ startups.
**Plano (~15 min):** **Toyota North America HQ**, Texas Instruments, Frito-Lay, JCPenney, Liberty Mutual, fintech firms.

Play: engineered travel-friendly trays (jollof + suya + protein + sides), fast quotes, direct ordering (not marketplace), and office-manager outreach + sample drops. Corporate catering is the steady revenue floor; dine-in and Friday sit on top.

## The Friday wedge (Craig's night = the real opening)

**The white space:** every recurring Afrobeats/Amapiano Friday in the metro is **downtown or in Dallas/Carrollton** — Zodiac (Afrobeats Fridays), Portico Lounge, Sandaga 813 (**True Colors**, founded by **DJ Ade** — @djadeofficial, already in M&M's performer pipeline), Canvas rooftop (AfroSplash). **There is no dedicated Afrobeats Friday in Frisco/Plano.** The affluent northern African crowd drives 30–45 min south for it — and True Colors itself was founded *because* North Texas clubs felt overpriced and reserved-section-heavy for young Africans.

**M&M's Friday can own the night the north side doesn't have** — closer, no downtown drive, food-first, less pressure. Craig's residency is the flywheel hub: a repeatable weekly beat, co-promoted by whoever plays, cross-linked to Shebelle's Sunday (see two-venue circuit), and — because Craig is on 5% — directly his upside. **Locking @djadeofficial (DJ Ade / True Colors) would import the exact target crowd in one move.**

⚠️ **Sequencing caution:** the food reputation is still young. Keep **food / family / catering as the base and Friday as the one nightlife tentpole** — do not over-drive club energy before the review base and first-visit confidence are set, or M&M risks the "more club than family" trap that dogs Shebelle. Frisco is family-first.
