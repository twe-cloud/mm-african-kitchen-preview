## Pillar 2 · Pack every night — a daypart machine (not a Friday show)

The 5% is on *sales*, all week, so NB content rotates across a reason for **every** daypart. Craig's Friday is **one beat, not the spine.** (Grid to confirm against M&M's actual programming.)

| Daypart | Reason to come | NB beat / CTA |
|---|---|---|
| **Weekday lunch + all-week** | **Corporate catering / office trays** (Frisco–Plano HQs) — the high-margin floor | Nia "the move" + catering CTA |
| **Happy hour Tue–Fri 3–7** | After-work West African happy hour (no downtown drive) | Ada culture cut + happy-hour offer |
| **Wed** | Karaoke / open mic (infra already built) | "Play the room" performer co-promo |
| **Thu** | Date-night / RnB / group dinner | Ada + reservation CTA |
| **Fri** | Afrobeats night — the one nightlife tentpole (one beat) | Ada Afrobeats + DJ co-promo repost |
| **Sat** | Celebrations, private events, big group tables | Sipho watch-party (when sports land) + private-event CTA |
| **Sun** | Family / brunch | Kevo "taste of home" + family CTA |

**Bias content toward measurable, high-ticket occasions** — catering, group bookings, packed-night reservations — because 5% compounds fastest there. A booked Tuesday catering order can beat a hyped Friday for influenced revenue.

**Sequencing caution (Frisco is family-first):** food / family / catering is the base; Friday grows on top. Do not over-drive club energy before the review base and first-visit confidence are set (avoid the "more club than family" trap).

## Pillar 3 · The 5% attribution layer (this is the paycheck)

NB earns on *influenced* sales, so proving influence is first-class — it's how we invoice, and it doubles as NB's sponsor-value proof.

- **Tracked `/go/mm-*` paths** on every NB post, GBP button, and Telegram drop — subId = channel → M&M order/reserve path. Clicks + conversions become countable.
  - Suggested slugs: `/go/mm-catering`, `/go/mm-reserve`, `/go/mm-friday`, `/go/mm-happyhour`, `/go/mm-private`, `/go/mm-menu`.
- **Mention / offer codes at the counter** ("say the code") tie *offline* sales to NB influence — the part clicks miss.
- **Loyalty program = the attribution bridge.** M&M already runs "every dollar a point." NB-driven signups get tagged; their spend is provably influenced.
- **QR** on any NB content/flyer → tracked landing.
- **Weekly influenced-sales report** = the 5% invoice basis *and* the proof that keeps M&M a paying NB sponsor.

- **Week 4 — measure & double down:** first weekly influenced-sales report; rank daypart/beat by influenced revenue; double down on the winner, rewrite the laggard.
