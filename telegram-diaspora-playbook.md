## Why Telegram for a restaurant
Telegram/WhatsApp groups are where the DFW African diaspora actually **coordinates** — churches, professional associations, community and events channels. That's where **catering leads and big-table bookings** get sourced (birthdays, professional meetups, church events, watch parties). It's high-intent, close-knit, and link-friendly — and it plugs straight into NB's existing attribution rails, so every drop is countable toward the 5%.

## Attribution scheme (this is the point)
Every Telegram link carries channel attribution, exactly like the NB Telegram Sales Matrix:
- Format: `shop.nibiashara.biz/go/<slug>?ch=telegram` (e.g. `/go/mm-catering?ch=telegram`).
- `/go` logs the click to GA4; conversions log by `subId2=channel`; `money_feedback.py` ranks drops by clicks → conversions → earnings-per-click.
- → Telegram-driven M&M sales become **provable influenced revenue** (the 5% invoice basis) and self-correcting ranked data.

## Synergy — SIQ turns nights into sellable inventory
If **@SIQDallas** gets built (NB's diaspora events arm), M&M/Shebelle nights become inventory SIQ can sell — group passes, reserved tables — via **Stripe checkout** with full `?ch=telegram` attribution. NB's events channel + M&M the venue + Telegram the sales rail = monetizable *and* measurable group bookings. (SIQ's payment path is blocked only on a human BotFather/Stripe-token step; organic tracked-link drops need none of that and are ready today.)

## Cadence & etiquette
- **Value-first, not spam.** A useful drop (catering, an event people want, a new dish) 1–2× per channel per week, max. Respect each group's rules; being removed for spam kills the channel permanently.
- Lead with the food/occasion, link second.
- Vary the copy per channel; don't paste the identical message everywhere.
- Track which channels convert (via `?ch=telegram` + slug) and concentrate effort on winners.

## Guardrails
- Organic / $0. No payment-bot setup required for M&M promotion (that's separate, human-gated NB work).
- Strategy-only until same-session approval to actually post/join/drop.
- M&M owns the relationships and data; Ni Biashara operates scoped.
- `@mototwe` / personal channels are out of scope.
- Truthful attribution: a sale is "influenced" only when the tracked path or code actually connects it.
