How it works

Give your name or phone at the counter. That's it.

The counter team logs your visit and your spend. Points accumulate across all orders — dine-in, takeout, and catering. Ask your current balance any time you visit.

Redeem

When you hit a tier, tell the counter team what you want to redeem. Points are deducted and the reward is applied to that visit.

Points do not expire. Rewards cannot be combined with monthly preorder specials.
