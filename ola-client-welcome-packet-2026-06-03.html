Access rule

Use delegated access where possible.

The best setup is not password sharing. The best setup is account ownership with scoped access, clear roles, and the ability for M&M to revoke or adjust access anytime.

04 / KPIs

What we will measure and why it matters.

The point is not to bury you in dashboards. The point is to show what changed, what worked, what is blocked, and where the next dollar or saved hour may come from.

Proof, not noise
KPI What it tells us Review cadence
Website traffic Whether more people are reaching the M&M website after link/profile cleanup. Weekly after measurement is active.
Calls, directions, and order taps Whether visitors are taking real customer actions, not just browsing. Weekly.
Catering and event inquiries Whether the site is creating higher-value opportunities beyond normal walk-in traffic. Weekly and month end.
Revenue impact The best reasonable estimate of business value connected to the pilot work. Month-end review.
Owner response time How quickly requests, approvals, and blockers move through the owner lane. Weekly.
Control readiness Whether Cloudflare, mailboxes, Drive, tracker, and domain records are clean enough for handoff. Month-end handoff.

The signed pilot amount is capped.

The pilot uses a simple placeholder amount so we can put an operational number on the month without creating a heavy fee structure. It is the lower of 1% of reasonably estimated business impact attributed to the project work or $50. It never goes over $50 under the signed pilot agreement.

1% of attributed impact or $50 cap whichever is less

That $50 also keeps the domain and operating record clean for M&M: ownership stays with the business, the handoff path is documented, and any deeper future commercial terms must be separately discussed and signed.

05 / Brunch + talent approvals

One programming decision unlocks the outreach list.

We should not start outreach with mixed signals. Please choose the first public brunch lane, then approve the starting outreach targets and budget band.

Owner decision needed
Option A

Activate a Monday teaser / taster short list.

Use a lighter interest-list campaign first: social posts, WhatsApp/share link, and a short signup list for people who want first notice when tasting seats or preview dates open.

  • Best if we want signal before announcing the full brunch concept.
  • Lower commitment: no hard date, no menu promise, no DJ booking until demand is visible.
  • Useful for collecting names, phone/email, preferred day, party size, and interest level.
Option B

Turn the Sunday smoked-turkey teaser back on.

Restore the stronger Sunday brunch concept as originally requested: smoked turkey, cocktails, music, influencer preview, and a clear waitlist CTA.

  • Best if Ola wants to make a bigger public move quickly.
  • Needs owner-approved launch date, menu, seating capacity, pricing, and talent budget.
  • Requires faster outreach to DJs, performers, creators, and possibly a blogger brunch partner.
